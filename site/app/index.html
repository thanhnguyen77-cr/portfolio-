What runs here

This page runs the same Python scripts the Claude Code plugin uses, inside your browser: the market regime check, the screener, the backtester with fees and out-of-sample checks, the capped Kelly sizer, the risk check and the paper broker. Nothing is sent to a server.

One step is different. In Claude Code an AI analyst agent writes the trade plan. Here a rule-based planner follows the same written rules: a stop below recent structure and at least 1.5 ATR away, a target at your minimum reward to risk, and one of the four setups the backtester can test. The AI replay tab shows the real agents at work.

Data

Latest: daily bars for the watchlist plus SPY, QQQ and VIX, refreshed by this site's GitHub Actions workflow each weekday after the US close using the open-source yfinance library. Prices are split and dividend adjusted. This is end-of-day data, not a live feed.

2012 to 2017: real daily prices for 20 US stocks from the stocknet dataset, SPY rebuilt from zipline's daily return series, and VIX from datasets/finance-vix, all loaded straight from GitHub.

Chart advice

For any stock on the watchlist, the plan is drawn on a candlestick chart with the 20, 50 and 200 day averages. The timing instructions follow the desk's rules: entries are placed before the next session's open, as a limit or a buy stop-limit, and expire if not filled that day. The sliders change only the stop and the target; the shares, loss, gain and expected value update from the same sizing caps the desk uses.

CFD

The CFD page costs out two kinds of arbitrage on quotes you enter: a cross-broker gap and a CFD-versus-stock basis trade. It subtracts spreads, commissions and financing and shows the net per share. It is a calculator, not a feed: no free source publishes live CFD quotes.

Simulate

The desk runs on a schedule you choose. Each time it can place one bracket order (entry, stop and target), sized by the rules above. Orders fill the next trading day against real bars. Fill rules are conservative: if a day touches both stop and target, the stop counts. Positions still open after 20 sessions are closed at the next open.

Not advice

This is an educational tool. Past results do not predict future ones, and no order here reaches a real broker.
