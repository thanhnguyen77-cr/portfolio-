Caveats

This report describes the past under stated assumptions. It is not a claim that anything is profitable or error-free.

  • Risk measures are estimated from the sample shown; they describe that history and will be wrong about the next crisis in ways the sample cannot reveal.
  • Portfolio returns hold the given weights constant (daily rebalancing, no costs), which overstates diversification relative to a drifting buy-and-hold book.
  • VaR/CVaR at 99% over 1 day(s); parametric and simulation methods scale with the square root of time and assume iid returns.
  • SYNTHETIC DATA: every price, action and macro observation in this dataset was generated from a seeded model. Results describe the generator, not any market.
  • Only 3/8 stress windows fall inside the available price history.
