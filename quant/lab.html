Simplified. This page re-runs the rule in your browser: a signal at one close is traded at the next close, with a flat cost per trade. The full platform engine fills at the next open with spreads, slippage, stamp duty and per-currency cash, so its numbers differ.

Not advice. Three hand-picked large companies that survived and grew make any rule look better than it would on a fair universe. Trying many settings and keeping the best one overstates what it would earn next.
